Reading Question Types

Introduce the four question types in this reading: detail, main idea, vocab in context, inference/attitude.

Question Type: Detail

DoFind the exact fact in the text.
WhyThe answer is written there.
HowFind the key words. Scan for them.
Explain the question type. Give a PET-style example orally if needed.

Try it: Detail

Lily lives in Chiang Mai. It rains most in August.
Which month is wettest?
 May
 August
 December

Question Type: Main Idea

DoAsk: What is this about?
WhyIt shows the big picture.
HowRead the first sentence of each paragraph.

Question Type: Vocabulary in Context

DoGuess the meaning of the word.
WhyThe sentence gives clues.
HowLook at the words before and after.

Question Type: Inference & Attitude

DoRead between the lines.
WhySome answers are not written directly.
HowThink: What does this really mean?
Explain the question type. Give a PET-style example orally if needed.

Try it: Inference

Many students ride bikes to school. There are only a few parking spaces for cars.
What does the writer suggest?
 The school is very far from home.
 Bikes are faster than cars.
 Most students do not drive.

Drill the Opinion-Reason-Evidence frame with the example. The yellow O-R-E label is required on every discussion-structure slide.

Agree, Disagree, Resolve

AgreeI agree with you because…
DisagreeI see your point, but…
ResolutionSo we both think… Let’s agree on that.
Interaction language: agree, disagree politely, then resolve.
