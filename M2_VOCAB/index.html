Why is this lesson important?

  • You see these words in the news every day.
  • They help you understand stories about the world.
  • They help you talk about how people help.

The Green Garden Project

Transition to listening. Say: We will listen to a radio interview about a community garden.

Strategy: Predict

DoPredict what Tom will say.
WhyIt helps your brain focus.
HowRead the questions. Think first.
Say: The title is The Green Garden Project. A volunteer talks on the radio. What might he talk about? Predict first, then listen.

Try it: Predict

A volunteer talks on the radio about his project.
Which topics might he mention?
 His favorite food.
 A movie he watched.
 The community garden.
 His neighbors.
Ask students to predict which topics a volunteer might mention. Click to reveal the checkmarks.

Revealed: the garden and his neighbors make sense. Food and movies are less likely for a radio talk about a project.

Listen — Gist

What is the Green Garden Project?

Gist — Answer

QuestionWhat is the Green Garden Project?
AnswerIt's a charity. It turned an old wasteland into a garden for the community.
ExplanationTom says the project is a charity in his area that made a garden for people.
Transcript“It's a charity in our area. We turned an old wasteland into a garden.”
Confirm the gist. The project is a community charity garden.

Listen again

Answer the questions.

1. How did Tom first get involved?

2. What does the garden give the community?

3. What is the fundraising campaign for?

4. What did a local company do?

Answers (1/4)

Question1. How did Tom first get involved?
AnswerA neighbor encouraged him to join.
ExplanationHe got to know the people in his area through the project.
Transcript“A neighbor encouraged me to join.”
Check the answer. Point out the words neighbor and encourage from the unit.

Answers (2/4)

Question2. What does the garden give the community?
AnswerA safe place for children to play and fresh food for some families.
ExplanationChildren play there now, and the garden gives fresh food to families in poverty.
Transcript“Children play there now.” “The garden gives them fresh food.”
Check the answer. Point out the words poverty and hunger from the vocabulary.

Answers (3/4)

Question3. What is the fundraising campaign for?
AnswerTo buy more plants and equipment.
ExplanationTom says the money will buy what the garden needs to grow.
Transcript“We want to buy more plants and equipment.”
Check the answer. Point out the word fundraising from the unit.

Answers (4/4)

Question4. What did a local company do?
AnswerIt donated a large sum of money.
ExplanationThe company gave money to help the project succeed.
Transcript“A local company just donated a large sum of money.”
Check the answer. Point out the word donate from the unit.
