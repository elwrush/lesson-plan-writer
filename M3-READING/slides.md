In the past, people feared sea monsters and the unknown.

**How much do we know?**

Only 5% of the ocean has been mapped — we know Mars better.

**Creatures of the deep**

Goblin sharks, vampire squids, and giant tube worms live in the deep sea.

#  {#slide-auto-4 data-autoslide="2500" data-transition="slide-in slide-out"}

**Deep-sea geography**

Challenger Deep is 11,000 m deep — the Mid-Atlantic Ridge stretches 70,000 km.

#  {#slide-auto-5 data-autoslide="2500" data-transition="slide-in slide-out"}

**Why should we care?**

The deep sea produces 70% of our oxygen and regulates Earth's temperature.
