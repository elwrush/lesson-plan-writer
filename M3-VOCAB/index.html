Listen — Gist

What is Leo doing?

Gist — Answer

QuestionWhat is Leo doing?
AnswerHe is doing a survey project about numbers.
ExplanationHe measures things and asks students about homework.
Transcript“I've been measuring things around school.”
Confirm the gist. Leo is collecting data for a survey project.

Listen again

Answer the questions.

1. How long does the average student spend on homework?

2. What percentage of students said that?

3. What are the classroom measurements?

4. How does Leo organize his numbers?

Answers (1/4)

Question1. How long does the average student spend on homework?
AnswerAbout an hour every night — that's the average.
ExplanationLeo found the average was higher than he expected.
Transcript“The average student spends about an hour on homework every night.”
Check the answer. Point out the word average from the vocabulary.

Answers (2/4)

Question2. What percentage of students said that?
AnswerAround sixty percent.
ExplanationMost of the students Leo asked agreed.
Transcript“Around sixty percent.”
Check the answer. Point out the word percentage from the vocabulary.

Answers (3/4)

Question3. What are the classroom measurements?
AnswerNine meters long, seven meters wide, and about three meters high.
ExplanationLeo measured the length, width, and height himself.
Transcript“The length of our classroom comes to nine meters, while the width is just seven.”
Check the answer. Point out length, width, and height from the vocabulary.

Answers (4/4)

Question4. How does Leo organize his numbers?
AnswerHe puts them in a sequence, from smallest to largest.
ExplanationOrdering the data makes it easier to compare.
Transcript“I put them in a sequence, from smallest to largest.”
Check the answer. Point out the word sequence from the vocabulary.
