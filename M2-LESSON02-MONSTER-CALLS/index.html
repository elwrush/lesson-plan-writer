Lily’s Version

Lily: “Stop it, Harry! Leave him alone!”

Harry: “Stay out of it, Lily.”

Lily: “He did not do anything to you!”

Harry: “He exists. That’s enough.”

Lily: (shouting) “MISS KWAN! MISS KWAN!”

Read aloud. Elicit: “How does Lily feel? How do we know? The words she uses.”

Script Format Guide

Character Name: Dialogue line here.

Another Character: (stage direction) Dialogue continues.

Harry: (cold smile) He exists. That's enough.

Keep lines short: 5–10 words per line.

Walk through the format. Every line shows who speaks and what they say.
