# Script Format Guide {#script-format}

```
Character Name: Dialogue line here.

Another Character: (stage direction) Dialogue continues.

Harry: (cold smile) He exists. That's enough.
```

Keep lines short: [5--10 words]{.box} per line.

::: notes
Walk through the format. Every line shows who speaks and what they say.
:::
