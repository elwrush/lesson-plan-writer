Strategy: Select and order

DoChoose the strongest ideas.
WhyYou have space for only two or three.
HowDecide the order. Join ideas in one sentence.
Page 134, Writing strategy. Start or finish with the strongest point.

QuestionWhich arguments are joined in one sentence?
AnswerParagraph 2: "Second, you can create your own playlists, and what's more, share them with friends easily." Paragraph 3: "There's also the fact that some artists don't appear on streaming services, and another argument against them is that they don't pay artists much."
ExplanationLinking phrases join two ideas in one sentence.
Transcript"what's more" and "another argument against them is"
