B1 Answers (1/8)

Question1. According to the company, how many people played Roblox in 2024?
AnswerB. Over 85 million people used it daily.
ExplanationThe company reports that more than 85 million people played every day. The other options mix up the numbers, like the 40% who were under 13.
Transcript“In 2024, more than 85 million people played it every day, and about 40% of them were under 13.”

B1 Answers (2/8)

Question2. What did the researchers discover about the accounts of young children?
AnswerC. Adults could message even the youngest accounts.
ExplanationThe researchers made fake accounts for pretend children. Even the five-year-old account could chat with adults. The platform could not tell how old anyone was.
Transcript“Children as young as five could chat with adults while playing.”

B1 Answers (3/8)

Question3. What is the writer's purpose in writing this passage?
AnswerD. To show how easily children meet danger there.
ExplanationThe middle of the text describes the experiment and what the fake accounts could access. The writer uses these details to show that real dangers hide behind the friendly look.
Transcript“The ten-year-old's account could enter rooms that were clearly not suitable for children.”

B1 Answers (4/8)

Question4. What is the main idea of paragraph 7?
AnswerC. The fixes still leave children exposed to strangers.
ExplanationThe paragraph says the new safety features do not go far enough. Children can still talk to strangers who are not on their friends list.
Transcript“Children can still talk to strangers who are not on their friends list.”

B1 Answers (5/8)

Question5. In paragraph 4, what does the word “verification” mean?
AnswerA. Checking that information is true and correct.
ExplanationAge verification means proving that information, such as a player's age, is true. The text says no real verification existed, so nobody could check how old players were.
Transcript“It was impossible to know how old any player really was.”

B1 Answers (6/8)

Question6. What does the passage suggest about Roblox's voice chat protection?
AnswerD. Players can get around it without much difficulty.
ExplanationThe report says the AI checks are easy to avoid. The researchers found that the filters did not catch the adult's request.
Transcript“The report says such measures are easy to avoid.”

B1 Answers (7/8)

Question7. Many players are children, but the age checks fail. What does the writer suggest?
AnswerB. An adult can easily pretend to be a child.
ExplanationBecause there is no real way to check age, nobody can tell which players are adults and which are children. An adult can simply create an account and look like a young player.
Transcript“An adult account asked a five-year-old's account for personal details.”

B1 Answers (8/8)

Question8. How does the writer feel about Roblox in the final paragraphs?
AnswerA. Critical of the company's efforts so far.
ExplanationThe writer ends by saying the company must do far more. That is a critical tone: the writer believes the efforts so far are not enough.
Transcript“It is time for the company to do far more, or for parents to think twice.”
