Reading Question Types

Introduce the four question types in this reading: detail, main idea, vocab in context, inference/attitude.

Question Type: Detail

DoFind the exact fact in the text.
WhyThe answer is written there.
HowFind the key words. Scan for them.
Explain the question type. Give a PET-style example orally if needed.

Try it: Detail

Nina works at a city hospital. She starts at seven in the morning.
Where does Nina work?
 At a hospital
 At a school
 At a bank
