Let's get Started

Quick review: What three things does a complete sentence need?

Look at this sentence:

My roommate lost his keys.

  • Subject: Who lost the keys? My roommate
  • Verb: What did he do? lost
  • Object: What did he lose? his keys

Today we practice finding S, V, and O — so we can write better sentences!
