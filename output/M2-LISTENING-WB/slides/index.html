Listen for the Main Ideas

Listening for Main Ideas

When you listen for the main idea, ask yourself:

  • Who is talking, and what about?
  • What is the conversation mostly about?
  • Don't worry about every word — listen for the big picture.

Want a challenge? Listen first without looking at the questions. Take notes, then try to answer from your notes.

Useful Phrases

Useful Phrases for Advice

These phrases help you give advice about online safety:

  • be + adjective (be careful)
  • make sure + that clause (make sure that the website is safe)
  • be sure to + infinitive (be sure to look)
  • keep an eye + open/out for (keep an eye open for offers)
  • take care + to-infinitive (take care to use trusted stores)

Let's Talk

Structure Your Talk

Thesis: Say your main idea about the topic

Reasons: Give 1-2 reasons or tips

Example: Share an example the class will understand

First...   Also...   For example...

Want your group to go further? Add a strong conclusion that summarises your main point.
