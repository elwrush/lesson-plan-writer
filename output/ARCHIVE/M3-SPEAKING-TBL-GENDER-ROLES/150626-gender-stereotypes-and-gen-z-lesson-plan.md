## Stage 1: Concept Check CCQ

**Time:** 5 min  |  **Interaction:** T-Ss

**Aim:** To activate learners' recall of key statistical evidence about Gen Z attitudes

- 5 slide-based CCQs on the Kings College study statistics
- Focus on key figures: 31% Gen Z men, 33% final word, 61% enough done
- Pairs discuss their answers
- Whole-class feedback

## Stage 2: Video Input — Andrew Tate

**Time:** 5 min  |  **Interaction:** T-Ss

**Aim:** To introduce a second source of evidence for the speaking task

- Show Andrew Tate YouTube clip
- Present stats slide on his media reach and Gen Z target audience
- Brief class discussion: why does this matter for our debate?

## Stage 3: Structure Input

**Time:** 10 min  |  **Interaction:** T-Ss

**Aim:** To present and model the Opinion → Reasons → Evidence monolog structure

- Display the four-part structure: Opinion, Reason 1, Reason 2, Conclusion
- Model how to extract WH evidence from the BTN video and Tate clip
- Emphasise non-verbose outline format: bullet points only, no full sentences
- Show example outline on slide

## Stage 4: Transitions Input

**Time:** 5 min  |  **Interaction:** T-Ss

**Aim:** To introduce appropriate discourse markers for each part of the monolog

- Display transition phrases on slide
- Opinion openers: I partly agree/disagree that..., In my view...
- Evidence phrases: According to the BTN video..., The study found that...
- Concluding phrases: Overall, this suggests that..., Taking everything into account...

## Stage 5: Model Answer Analysis

**Time:** 5 min  |  **Interaction:** T-Ss

**Aim:** To analyse a model answer that demonstrates the opinion-evidence-conclusion structure

- Display model answer on slide
- Students identify: opinion, reason 1, reason 2, conclusion
- Draw attention to discourse markers linking each part
- Elicit what makes the evidence effective

## Stage 6: Task Cycle — Prepare Outline

**Time:** 10 min  |  **Interaction:** S

**Aim:** To give learners time to prepare their own structured outline for the monolog

- Students write a non-verbose outline for their 2-minute monolog
- Outline must include: opinion, 2 reasons, WH evidence from BTN/Tate, conclusion
- Teacher circulates and monitors, providing guidance on structure and evidence

## Stage 7: Task Cycle — Individual Monolog

**Time:** 6 min  |  **Interaction:** S

**Aim:** To give learners the opportunity to deliver their monolog individually

- Students deliver their 2-minute monolog to the teacher individually
- Speaking without notes from their outline
- Teacher observes and notes strengths in structure, transitions, and evidence
- Formal PET-scale assessments in subsequent lessons
