= Lesson Stages

## Stage 1: Lead-in — Balance Beam Discussion

**Time:** 3 min  |  **Interaction:** T-Ss

**Aim:** To activate thinking about how small, meticulous adjustments lead to big improvements

- Show the image of Suni Lee on the balance beam
- Ask: "What does it take to stay on the balance beam?"
- Elicit: focus, tiny adjustments, listening to your coach's feedback
- Ask: "How is this like writing?"
- Elicit: you read your teacher's feedback, you fix small things — a comma here, a complete sentence there — and your score improves
- Bridge: "Today you get your feedback back. Every small shift you make will bring you closer to your best score."

## Stage 2: Feedback Report Tour

**Time:** 2 min  |  **Interaction:** T-Ss

**Aim:** To familiarise students with the four scoring areas of their feedback reports

- Show the slide listing Content, Communicative Achievement, Organisation, and Language
- Walk students through each section briefly
- Emphasise: "Today we focus on the Language section — that's where small fixes make the biggest difference."
- Do NOT read out any individual student's scores

## Stage 3: Common Errors — Punctuation

**Time:** 3 min  |  **Interaction:** T-Ss

**Aim:** To highlight and correct the common error of placing spaces before punctuation marks

- Show Slide 5 (auto-animate entry): "friends at school , so if you be that how would you feel ?"
- Ask: "Can you see anything wrong with the punctuation?"
- Advance to Slide 6 (auto-animate reveal): corrected version with the rule displayed below
- Emphasise: spaces go after punctuation, not before

## Stage 4: Common Errors — Sentence Fragments

**Time:** 3 min  |  **Interaction:** T-Ss

**Aim:** To show how incomplete sentences (fragments) can be expanded into complete sentences

- Show Slide 7 (auto-animate entry): "raised and taught differently and inspired or influenced differently"
- Ask: "Is this a complete sentence? What's missing?" (subject + finite verb)
- Advance to Slide 8 (auto-animate reveal): corrected version with the rule displayed below
- Remind: every sentence needs a subject and a verb

## Stage 5: Independent Rewriting

**Time:** 30 min  |  **Interaction:** Ss (individual)

**Aim:** To apply feedback by rewriting the article draft

- Hand out printed feedback reports (one per student with their original writing attached)
- Show Slide 9 with instructions: "Read your feedback. Then rewrite your article."
- Circulate and offer individual support
- Collect revised drafts at the end

## Stage 6: Wrap-up

**Time:** 5 min  |  **Interaction:** T-Ss

**Aim:** To consolidate learning and celebrate improvement

- Ask 2-3 students: "What was one thing you improved in your rewrite?"
- Remind: "Every small shift — fixing one comma, completing one sentence — makes your writing stronger"
- Collect final drafts
