# Your Feedback Report

Every report scores your article across **four areas**:

- **Content** — Did you answer the question? Did you develop your ideas?
- **Communicative Achievement** — Does your writing feel natural? Can you hold the reader's attention?
- **Organisation** — Is it structured well? Paragraphs, linking words, clear flow.
- **Language** — Grammar, vocabulary, punctuation — the small details that add up.

::: notes
Walk students through the four sections of their feedback report. "Each section has a score and specific comments. Today we focus on the Language section — that's where small fixes make the biggest difference." Time: 2 min. T-Ss.
:::

# Common Errors

#  {data-auto-animate="true" data-auto-animate-id="punct-demo"}

[friends at]{data-id=t1} [school , so]{data-id=t2} [if you be that how would you]{data-id=t3} [feel ?]{data-id=t4 style="white-space: nowrap;"}

[Spaces go **after** punctuation — not before.]{style="visibility: hidden;"}

::: notes
"Look at this sentence from someone's draft. Can you see anything odd about the punctuation?" Elicit: spaces before the comma and question mark. Time: 1 min. T-Ss.
:::

#  {data-auto-animate="true" data-auto-animate-id="punct-demo"}

[friends at]{data-id=t1} [school, so]{data-id=t2 style="border: 2px solid #ffdd00; border-radius: 4px; padding: 2px 8px;"} [if you be that how would you]{data-id=t3} [feel?]{data-id=t4 style="border: 2px solid #ffdd00; border-radius: 4px; padding: 2px 8px; white-space: nowrap;"}

[Spaces go **after** punctuation — not before.]{style="color: #ffdd00;"}

::: notes
"Now the comma and question mark are right where they belong — directly after the word, followed by a space. Never put a space before punctuation." Time: 1 min. T-Ss.
:::

#  {data-auto-animate="true" data-auto-animate-id="frag-demo"}

[raised and taught differently and inspired or influenced differently]{data-id=subj}

[Every sentence needs a **subject** and a **verb**.]{style="visibility: hidden;"}

::: notes
"Here's another real example. Listen: 'raised and taught differently and inspired or influenced differently' — does that sound like a complete sentence?" Elicit: it's a fragment, missing a subject (who?) and a finite verb. Time: 1 min. T-Ss.
:::

#  {data-auto-animate="true" data-auto-animate-id="frag-demo"}

[These teenagers]{data-id=subj style="border: 2px solid #ffdd00; border-radius: 4px; padding: 2px 8px;"} [were raised differently, taught differently, and inspired or influenced by different things.]{data-id=rest style="border: 2px solid #ffdd00; border-radius: 4px; padding: 2px 8px;"}

[Every sentence needs a **subject** and a **verb**.]{style="color: #ffdd00;"}

::: notes
"Now it's a complete sentence. 'These teenagers' is the subject, 'were raised...taught...inspired' is the verb group. Every sentence needs both." Time: 1 min. T-Ss.
:::
