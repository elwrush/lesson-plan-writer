# Lesson Stages

## Stage 1: Discussion

**Time:** 5 min  |  **Interaction:** Pairs → T-Ss

**Aim:** To activate interest in the topic of business success

- Pairs discuss: How can you be successful in business?
- Elicit 4–5 ideas and write them on the board
- Introduce the infographic tips from p. 44 (check emails a few times a day, eat a healthy breakfast, plan in minutes, do the hardest tasks first)
- Ask: Which of these do you agree with? Anything you would add?
- Transition: We are going to watch a video about young entrepreneurs in China and see how they became successful

## Stage 2: Video — Three-Level Challenge

**Time:** 10 min  |  **Interaction:** Indiv → Pair → T-Ss

**Aim:** To listen for specific information about young entrepreneurs in China

- Explain the three-level challenge format before playing the video
- **Level 1** (stronger Ss): Answer the While you watch questions (p. 45) using the handout to read along while watching
- **Level 2** (most Ss): Write notes while watching, then use the handout to check your answers after
- **Level 3** (challenge): Take no notes during the video, try to answer the questions from memory after the video ends
- Play the video once
- Ss pair check their answers regardless of level
- Whole-class feedback: confirm correct answers and clarify any confusion
- Quick discussion: Why do you think Song left his job? Is Cui Ernan right that entrepreneurship isn't for everyone?

## Stage 3: Vocabulary Preview

**Time:** 5 min  |  **Interaction:** Indiv → T-Ss

**Aim:** To ensure understanding of key vocabulary before reading

- Ss look at the word box: brand, customer service, excellence, logo, quality, trust, value
- Ask Ss to match each word to a simple definition or example
- Assist with any difficult words — check comprehension by asking concept questions:
  - *trust*: If you trust someone, do you believe them or not?
  - *logo*: Can you think of a famous logo? (Apple, Nike, etc.)
  - *customer service*: What happens when customer service is bad?
- Drill pronunciation of any challenging words
- Ss complete Ex. A1 (p. 46) for reinforcement

## Stage 4: Reading Challenge

**Time:** 16 min  |  **Interaction:** Indiv → Pairs → Group

**Aim:** To practise identifying main ideas and supporting details in a text using graduated challenge

- Explain the three-level format:
  - **Level 1** (stronger Ss): Keep Ex. C and D questions in front of you and read the text to find the answers
  - **Level 2** (most Ss): Read the text and take notes on the main ideas, then use your notes to answer Ex. C and D
  - **Level 3** (challenge): Read the text once, close it, and try to answer Ex. C and D from retention without looking back
- Ss read the text individually (p. 47) and work through Ex. C (p. 46 — match questions to paragraphs, predict word locations) and Ex. D (p. 48 — scan for supporting details, match to main ideas) at their chosen level
- Pairs: share answers and discuss any differences
- Whole-class feedback with text evidence for each answer
- Groups of 3: compare which details (1–6) support which main idea (Ex. D2) and justify using the text
- Quick reflection: Which level did you choose? Was it the right challenge for you?

## Stage 5: Critical Thinking

**Time:** 7 min  |  **Interaction:** Pairs → T-Ss

**Aim:** To apply critical thinking skills to evaluate ideas from the text and form reasoned opinions

- Model the critical thinking process for Ex. E1 (p. 48):
  - Step 1: Identify the three options (customer service, brand, quality products)
  - Step 2: Find evidence in the text for each one
  - Step 3: Decide which you think adds the MOST value and think of a reason WHY
  - Step 4: Consider the counter-argument — what might someone who disagrees say?
- T demonstrates with one option: *I think customer service adds the most value because the text says it's more expensive to attract new customers than to keep old ones. But someone might argue quality products are more important because people buy things for what they are, not just how they're treated.*
- In pairs, Ss choose their own option and follow the four steps
- Pairs share their reasoning with the class — encourage Ss to challenge each other politely
- Extension: Ex. E2 — What other things not mentioned add value? (company hours, happy employees, quick service)

## Stage 6: Wrap-up

**Time:** 3 min  |  **Interaction:** T-Ss

**Aim:** To consolidate key learning from the lesson

- Quick vocabulary recall: T shows a definition or example, Ss call out the word (brand, trust, logo, etc.)
- Ask 2–3 Ss: What is one thing you think adds value to a company? Use evidence from today's text
- Preview: Next lesson — Reading 2 "Starting on the path to success?" — we will look at what makes start-ups succeed or fail
