# B2 Challenge {#autocue-b2}

::: {.autocue .b2}
Arnott's is one of Australia's most famous food companies. It was founded in 1865 by Scottish immigrant William Arnott. He started by baking bread, but soon realised that biscuits lasted longer and could be transported further.

Today, Arnott's produces over 60 million biscuits every day. Its most famous product is the Tim Tam — a chocolate-covered biscuit that Australians love. The company has become a national icon.

What made Arnott's successful? First, quality — Arnott's always used good ingredients. Second, branding — the red Arnott's logo is recognised by 99% of Australians. Third, innovation — when they created Tim Tams in 1964, they invented a completely new product category.

The company also understood customer psychology. Arnott's biscuits were marketed as affordable treats during difficult economic times, which built strong emotional connections with customers that last for generations.

# How to Skim {#skim-intro}

**Three rules:**

1. Read the **first sentence** of each paragraph
2. Ask: *"What is this paragraph about?"*
3. **Match** each question to the paragraph with the same topic

::: notes
"Rule 1: first sentence tells you the topic of the paragraph. Rule 2: identify the topic. Rule 3: find which question matches that topic. You're not looking for the exact words — you're looking for the same idea." Time: 1 min. T-Ss.
:::

# Close Reading — How it works {#close-how}

**Supporting details** prove the main idea.

Look for signal phrases:

| Type | Signal phrases |
|------|--------------|
| Examples | such as, for instance, one example is |
| Reasons | because, due to, in order to |
| Explanations | in other words, which means |

::: notes
"Supporting details are the evidence that proves a main idea. How do you find them? Look for signal phrases — words that tell you a detail is coming. Such as, because, in other words — these are your clues." Time: 2 min. T-Ss.
:::

# Close Reading — Example {#close-example}

**Main idea:** [Regular exercise]{.box} is [good]{.box} for your [health]{.box}.

| Signal phrase | Type | Supporting detail |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **because** | Reason | reduces the risk of heart disease |
| **For instance** | Example | helps you sleep better |
| **In other words** | Explanation | improves concentration |

**Your turn — find the signal phrases in the text, then the details.**

::: notes
"See how the signal phrases from the table work in real sentences? Because introduces a reason, for instance introduces an example, in other words explains. Now in your text, find these signal phrases and the details that follow them." Time: 2 min. T-Ss.
:::

# Detail 1 {#detail-1}

[**rude customer service** (Par 2)]{.y}

You can return a faulty product, but bad service can't be returned.

*"...rude customer service, unlike a faulty product, cannot be returned"*

# Detail 2 {#detail-2}

[**new customers** (Par 2)]{.y}

It costs more to find new customers than to keep the ones you already have.

*"...the cost of attracting a new customer is much higher than the cost of keeping an existing one"*

# Detail 3 {#detail-3}

[**product** (Par 2)]{.y}

When companies sell similar things, good service is what makes them stand out.

*"...two or more companies are selling basically the same product"*

# Detail 4 {#detail-4}

[**good-quality** (Par 3)]{.y}

People buy quality products even when they don't strictly need them.

*"...people are willing to spend a lot of money on good-quality products even when they do not need them"*

# Detail 5 {#detail-5}

[**a watch** (Par 3)]{.y}

Watches are no longer essential for telling time, but people still buy them.

*"...A watch is no longer essential, so why are these expensive items still popular?"*

# Detail 6 {#detail-6}

[**who they are** (Par 4)]{.y}

People buy brands that match how they want to be seen by others.

*"...we like to buy brands that represent how we like to present ourselves to others"*

# What adds the most value? {#critical-thinking data-timer="300"}

1. **Identify** the three options
2. **Find** evidence in the text for each one
3. **Decide** which adds the most value — give a reason
