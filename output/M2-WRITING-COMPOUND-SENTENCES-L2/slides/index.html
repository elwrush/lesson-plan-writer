Two Sentence Errors

Two Sentence Errors

Comma splice — comma without a coordinator

My roommate wants to win the Tour de France, he spends hours riding his bicycle.

Run-on — nothing between two sentences

Write your signature on the line print your name below it.

"What's wrong? No coordinator after the comma. Nothing between 'line' and 'print'."

Two Sentence Errors

Comma splice — add a coordinator

My roommate wants to win the Tour de France, so he spends hours riding his bicycle.

Run-on — add a period

Write your signature on the line. Print your name below it.

"Now the comma splice is fixed — comma + so. The run-on is fixed — period + capital."

Two Ways to Fix

Method 1: Add a period. Capitalize the next word.

Wrong: "It's raining we can't go out."

Right: "It's raining. We can't go out."

Method 2: Add a comma. Add a coordinator (and, but, or, so).

Wrong: "It's raining we can't go out."

Right: "It's raining, so we can't go out."

Show both methods on the same wrong sentence. Students can use either method — both are correct.

Practice 11 — Answers (1–5)

1. Some people like cats, others prefer dogs. comma splice
Fix: "Some people like cats. Others prefer dogs."
2. Kittens are cute, they like to play. comma splice
Fix: "Kittens are cute. They like to play."
3. Dogs are good companions, and they can protect you. OK — correct
4. ...bark at strangers they shouldn't bite them, however. run-on
"strangers they" — nothing between. Fix: period after "strangers."
5. Lions are good protectors, but they eat too much. OK — correct
Item 4 has a tricky trailing "however" — it's a conjunctive adverb, not a coordinator, so it doesn't fix the run-on.

Practice 11 — Answers (6–10)

6. Penguins wear tuxedos, they are good pets for parties. comma splice
Fix: "...tuxedos. They are good pets..." or "...tuxedos, so they are..."
7. Elephant can fan you with his trunk, you won't need AC. comma splice
Fix: "trunk. You won't need..." or "trunk, so you won't..."
8. Goats eat grass, so you never have to cut your lawn. OK — correct
9. Giraffe can reach high shelves, it can see over heads. comma splice
Fix: "shelves. It can see..." or "shelves, and it can..."
10. Keep a boa constrictor ... alone then no one will visit. run-on
"alone then" — no connector. Fix: "alone. Then no one..."
Item 10: "then" is NOT a coordinator. Use Method 1 (period) or Method 2 (comma + and).
