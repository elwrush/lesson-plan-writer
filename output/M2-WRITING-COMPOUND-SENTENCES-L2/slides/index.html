Let's Not Make Two Common Sentence Errors

Error 1: The Comma Splice

Comma splice — comma without a coordinator

My friend is very kind, she always helps me with homework.

Re-display diagnostic item 1. "What's wrong? There's a comma, but where's the coordinator?" Elicit: comma splice.

Error 1: The Comma Splice

Comma splice — add a coordinator

My friend is very kind, so she always helps me with homework.

"Now the comma splice is fixed — comma + so. The coordinator 'so' connects reason and result." Also show Method 1: "My friend is very kind. She always helps me with homework."

Error 2: The Run-on

Run-on — nothing between two sentences

I go to school by bus it takes about thirty minutes.

Re-display diagnostic item 2. "What's wrong? Nothing at all between 'bus' and 'it'." Elicit: run-on.

Error 2: The Run-on

Run-on — add a period

I go to school by bus. It takes about thirty minutes.

"Now the run-on is fixed — period + capital letter." Also show Method 2: "I go to school by bus, and it takes about thirty minutes."

Two Ways to Fix

Method 1: Add a period. Capitalize the next word.

Wrong: "The food was delicious we ate everything on the table."

Right: "The food was delicious. We ate everything on the table."

Method 2: Add a comma + coordinator (and, but, or, so).

Wrong: "The food was delicious we ate everything on the table."

Right: "The food was delicious, so we ate everything on the table."

Show both methods on diagnostic item 4. Either method is correct. Method 1 uses a period. Method 2 keeps it as one sentence with comma + coordinator.

Practice 11 — Item 1

comma splice

"Some people like cats, others prefer dogs."

Method 1: Add a period

→ "Some people like cats. Others prefer dogs."

Method 2: Add a comma + coordinator

→ "Some people like cats, and others prefer dogs."

Why: Comma without a coordinator = comma splice. Both fixes are correct. Use meaning to choose — "and" (add) or "but" (contrast).

Show error badge first. Then Method 1 (period). Then Method 2 (comma + and). "And" or "but" both work here depending on meaning.

Practice 11 — Item 4

run-on

"It's acceptable for dogs to bark at strangers they shouldn't bite them, however."

Method 1: Add a period

→ "It's acceptable for dogs to bark at strangers. They shouldn't bite them, however."

Method 2: Add a comma + coordinator

→ "It's acceptable for dogs to bark at strangers, but they shouldn't bite them."

Why: "strangers they" — nothing between two sentences = run-on. ⚠ "However" is NOT a coordinator — it doesn't fix the run-on. M2 drops "however" (redundant with "but").

Critical item: 'however' is a conjunctive adverb, not a coordinator. Method 1 keeps 'however' at the end. Method 2 replaces it with 'but'.

Practice 11 — Item 5

OK — Correct

"Lions are also good protectors, but they eat too much."

Both methods: No fix needed — correct compound sentence.

Why: Already has comma + but = correct compound sentence (contrast). ✓

Item 5 is correct. Comma + but = correct compound sentence showing contrast.

comma splice

Method 2: Add a comma + coordinator

comma splice

Method 2: Add a comma + coordinator

Why: Comma without a coordinator = comma splice. Both fixes are correct. M2 uses "so" to show cause → effect.

Comma splice. Method 2 uses 'so' for cause-effect. The joke is that the elephant's trunk acts as a fan.

Practice 11 — Item 9

comma splice

"A giraffe can reach things on high shelves, it can see over the heads of people at parades."

Method 1: Add a period

→ "A giraffe can reach things on high shelves. It can see over the heads of people at parades."

Method 2: Add a comma + coordinator

→ "A giraffe can reach things on high shelves, and it can see over the heads of people at parades."

Why: Comma without a coordinator = comma splice. Both fixes are correct. M2 uses "and" to add information.

Practice 11 — Item 10

run-on

"Keep a boa constrictor as a pet if you enjoy being alone then no one will ever visit you."

Method 1: Add a period

→ "Keep a boa constrictor as a pet if you enjoy being alone. Then no one will ever visit you."

Method 2: Add a comma + coordinator

→ "Keep a boa constrictor as a pet if you enjoy being alone, and then no one will ever visit you."

Why: "alone then" — nothing between two sentences = run-on. ⚠ "Then" is NOT a coordinator — it doesn't fix the run-on alone. M2 uses "and then" (comma + coordinator + adverb).

Critical item: 'then' is not a coordinator. Method 1 (period) is the simplest fix. Method 2 needs 'and then' — 'and' does the coordinating, 'then' is just an adverb.
