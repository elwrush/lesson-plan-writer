Let's Not Make Two Common Sentence Errors

Error 1: The Comma Splice

Comma splice — comma without a coordinator

My roommate wants to win the Tour de France, he spends hours riding his bicycle.

"What's wrong? There's a comma, but where's the coordinator?"

Error 1: The Comma Splice

Comma splice — add a coordinator

My roommate wants to win the Tour de France, so he spends hours riding his bicycle.

"Now the comma splice is fixed — comma + so. The coordinator 'so' connects cause and effect."

Error 2: The Run-on

Run-on — nothing between two sentences

Write your signature on the line print your name below it.

"What's wrong? Nothing at all between 'line' and 'print'."

Error 2: The Run-on

Run-on — add a period

Write your signature on the line. Print your name below it.

"Now the run-on is fixed — period + capital letter."

Two Ways to Fix

Method 1: Add a period. Capitalize the next word.

Wrong: "It's raining we can't go out."

Right: "It's raining. We can't go out."

Method 2: Add a comma. Add a coordinator (and, but, or, so).

Wrong: "It's raining we can't go out."

Right: "It's raining, so we can't go out."

Show both methods on the same wrong sentence. Students can use either method — both are correct.

Practice 11 — Answers (1–3)

1. comma splice "Some people like cats. Others prefer dogs." Comma without coordinator. Fix: add a period (Method 1).
2. comma splice "Kittens are cute. They like to play." Comma without coordinator. Fix: add a period (Method 1).
3. OK "Dogs are good companions, and they can protect you." Comma + and = correct compound sentence. No fix needed.
Items 1-2: comma splice. Item 3: correct compound sentence with comma + and.

Practice 11 — Answers (4–6)

4. run-on "...bark at strangers. They shouldn't bite them, however." "strangers they" — nothing between two sentences. Fix: period (Method 1).
5. OK "Lions are good protectors, but they eat too much." Comma + but = correct compound sentence. No fix needed.
6. comma splice "Penguins wear tuxedos. They are good pets for parties." Comma without coordinator. Fix: period or comma + so.
Item 4: 'however' is a conjunctive adverb, not a coordinator — doesn't fix the run-on.

Practice 11 — Answers (7–8)

7. comma splice "Elephant can fan you with his trunk. You won't need AC." Comma without coordinator. Fix: period or comma + so.
8. OK "Goats eat grass, so you never have to cut your lawn." Comma + so = correct compound sentence. No fix needed.
Item 7: comma splice. Item 8: correct compound sentence.

Practice 11 — Answers (9–10)

9. comma splice "Giraffe can reach high shelves. It can see over heads." Comma without coordinator. Fix: period or comma + and.
10. run-on "Keep a boa constrictor ... alone. Then no one will visit." "alone then" — no connector. 'Then' is NOT a coordinator. Fix: period (Method 1).
Item 10: 'then' is not a coordinator. Use Method 1 (period) or Method 2 (comma + and).
