Social Media and Society B1

Read about how social media is changing the way we live

Lesson: Social Media and Society. B1 Reading. Shape E (Receptive Skills). 46 minutes. 7 stages: Lead-in, Vocabulary, Predicting, Gist, Detail, Discussion, Wrap-up. Materials: Skillful 2, Unit 1, pp 14-17.

Here's what you'll be able to do

  • I can get the main idea of an article
  • I can find important facts in a text
  • I can share my ideas about what I read

These are the same skills you need for your reading test!

Lesson objective. B1 reading sub-skills mapped to three outcomes. Connect to PET reading test framework. Elicit prior experience with social media topics.

Let's get Started

Is social media mostly good or mostly bad for our lives?

Stage 1 — Lead-in. 4 min. T-Ss, Ss-Ss. Write "Social media: positive or negative?" on the board. In pairs, students discuss how often they use social media and what they use it for. Elicit a few ideas and write key responses on the board. Introduce the lesson objective.

Finding details

Close Reading (p.17)

  • Read the article again carefully.
  • Write Y (Yes), N (No), or NG (Not Given) for each statement.
  • Find the evidence in the text for your answers.
Stage 5 — Reading for Detail. 12 min. S, Ss-Ss. Complete Y/N/NG exercise (D Close reading, p.17). Compare in pairs. Class feedback with text evidence.

Close Reading — Answers

D Close reading, p.17 — Y/N/NG

1.Social media is one of the main changes in modern society. Y
2.Hikikomori is just a Japanese problem. N
3.Teenagers are not addicted to social media.NG
4.Celebrity profiles show stars' real lives. N
Check one at a time. Ask: "Which paragraph has the evidence?" Read relevant sentences aloud.

Let's Discuss

Critical Thinking (p.17)

Do you agree or disagree? Give your reasons.

  • "Social media makes people feel anxious and depressed."
  • "Social media makes people want everything now."
Stage 6 — Discussion. 7 min. Ss-Ss. Groups discuss the two statements from E Critical thinking (p.17). Each group shares one key point. Encourage using article ideas.

What you can do now

  • I can get the main idea of an article

Stage 7 — Wrap-up. 3 min. T-Ss. Ask: "What was the most interesting argument?" and "Does the author think social media is more positive or negative?"
