Delivering a Podcast B2

A short talk to share your ideas and persuade your audience

Here's what you'll be able to do

  • Deliver a short podcast talk in 60 to 90 seconds
  • Use phrases to open, give reasons, and close your talk
  • Work with your partner using your chosen role

Let's get Started

What podcast topic did you and your partner plan last lesson?

Talk with your partner. You have 30 seconds to remember your plan.

Direct Ss to sit next to their podcast partner and pull out their planning sheet. Take 2 rapid-fire answers from pairs. Announce goal: record your podcast 60–90 seconds. One take. Baseline.

Choose Your Role

RoleStudent 1Student 2

B. Host + GuestOpens, asks 2 questionsAnswers with examples

Both partners speak 30–45 seconds. 30 seconds to choose.

Walk through each combination in 20 seconds. Directive: 30 seconds to decide. Default is A. Transition to phrases after selection.

Useful Phrases

Openings

“Welcome to my podcast…”   “Today I’m going to talk about…”

Giving reasons / advice

“Have you thought about…?”   “You might not realise this, but…”

Structuring

“First of all…”   “Another point is…”   “So, to sum up…”

Choral drilling: all-class repeat (1 round), rows (1 round). Emphasise natural conversational podcast rhythm. Ss annotate planning sheet: circle phrases for their combination.

Let's Practise

Mini-Monologue Drill

  • Round 1: You speak for 30 seconds. Use at least 2 phrases. Your partner counts.
  • Round 2: Switch. Your partner speaks for 30 seconds. You count.
Stage 3 · 5 min · Ss-Ss Goal: Low-stakes practice with target phrases before script writing. Stay with your assigned partner. Teacher highlights 1 strong example, corrects 1 common error (flat intonation).

Write Your Script

Write Your Script

  • ✓ Clear opening — who you are and what your topic is
  • ✓ At least 2 reasons or advice points
  • ✓ At least 3 useful phrases from the list
  • ✓ A short closing — “Thanks for listening”
  • ✓ 60–90 seconds when you speak it aloud

Use your planning sheet. Follow your role combination.

Stage 4 · 8 min · Ss-Ss Role-specific: A (S1 opening+arg1, S2 arg2+closing). B (S1 host intro+questions, S2 guest answers). C (S1 FOR, S2 AGAINST+closing). Countdown visible. Call time at 4 min and 1 min.

Rehearse

Rehearse

  • Round 1 (2 min): Practise with your script. Partner gives feedback.
  • Round 2 (1 min): Practise again. Try to look up and sound natural.

Partner: raise your hand when 60 seconds pass.

Stage 5 · 3 min · Ss-Ss Teacher moves between pairs giving rapid-fire coaching.

Recording Time

Recording Time

  • One take only. No restarts.
  • This is your starting point for the year.
  • Listen quietly while others record.

When your pair is called, come to the front and deliver your podcast.

Stage 6 · 20 min · Ss-Ss Teacher records at front. No feedback, corrections, or gestures during recording. Stagger pairs efficiently. ~90 seconds per pair (60-90s podcast + 10-20s transition). If all finish early: second take for pairs who feel first was unrepresentative. If time runs short: remaining pairs record next lesson.
