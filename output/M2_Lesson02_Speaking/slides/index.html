Online Safety B1

A short talk to share your ideas about staying safe online

Here's what you'll be able to do

  • Give a short talk about online safety in 60 seconds
  • Use a clear structure: main idea, reasons, and an example
  • Use emphasis phrases to make your talk stronger

Let's get Started

Tell your partner: one thing you remember about identity theft from last lesson.

You have 30 seconds. Then I'll ask 2 people to share.

Ss sit next to a partner from last lesson. Quick recall: 30 sec pair discussion. Take 2 rapid-fire answers. Announce goal: 60-second talk about online safety. One take. Baseline.

Structure Your Talk

Thesis: Say your main idea

Reasons: Give 1–2 reasons

Example: Share an example you know

First…   Also…   For example…

Review the structure from last lesson. This is the same framework used in the diagnostic. Ss should be familiar with it already.

Make Your Talk Stronger

Stating importance

“It’s really important to…”   “I want to say that…”

Giving strong advice

“You should always…”   “You should never…”

Making your point stronger

“The main thing is…”   “I strongly believe that…”

Model each phrase with an example: "It's really important to use strong passwords." Drill chorally (2 rounds) and individually (2-3 Ss). Ss note down 2-3 phrases they want to use.

Let's Practise

Mini-Talk

  • Think of one thing you do to stay safe online.
  • Talk for 30 seconds. Use at least one emphasis phrase.
  • Your partner listens and counts the phrases you use.

You have 1 minute to prepare, then 2 minutes each.

Stage 3 · 5 min · Ss-Ss Goal: Low-stakes practice with emphasis phrases. Ss prepare silently for 1 min, then deliver to partner (2 min each). Switch roles.

Write Your Script

Write Your Script

  • ✓ Clear thesis — your main idea about online safety
  • ✓ At least 1 reason to support your idea
  • ✓ A real example from your life or someone you know
  • ✓ At least 2 emphasis phrases from the lesson
  • ✓ A short closing sentence
  • ✓ 60 seconds when you speak it aloud

8 minutes. Use the structure from last lesson.

Stage 4 · 8 min · S Countdown timer visible. Circulate and help Ss who are stuck. Remind at 4 min and 1 min. Push for natural language.

Rehearse

Rehearse

  • Round 1 (2 min): Practise aloud with your script. Partner raises hand at 60 seconds.
  • Round 2 (1 min): Practise again. Try to look up and sound natural.

Time yourself. Make sure you hit 60 seconds.

Stage 5 · 3 min · S Teacher moves between Ss giving quick coaching.

Recording Time

Recording Time

  • One take only. No restarts.
  • This is your starting point for the year.
  • Listen quietly while others record.

When your name is called, come to the front and deliver your talk.

Stage 6 · 20 min · S Teacher records at front. No feedback, corrections, or gestures during recording. 18 Ss at ~65-70 seconds each fits in 20 min. If all finish early: second take for anyone who wants. If time runs short: remaining Ss record next lesson.

What was the hardest part? What went better than you expected?
