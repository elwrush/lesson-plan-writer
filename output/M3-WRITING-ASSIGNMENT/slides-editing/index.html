Editing Carousel B2

Good writing gets better

Let’s Edit

Today’s Objectives

1. I can self-edit my draft using a focused checklist.

2. I can peer-edit a classmate’s draft and give helpful feedback.

3. I can produce a final version with corrections from both rounds.

Stage aim: Set clear, measurable outcomes for the editing lesson.

Time: 1 min. Interaction: T-Ss.

Read each objective aloud. Explain that today is about improving writing, not writing something new.

Spot the Differences

Rough Draft
Edited Version
“I have a close friend her name is Sara. She always listens to me.” (run-on)
“I have a close friend named Sara, and she always listens to me.” (compound sentence)
“And makes me feel better.” (fragment)
“She makes me feel better when I am feeling down.” (complete sentence)
“Close friendships help teenagers feel less stressed they help you feel happier to.” (run-on)
“Close friendships help teenagers feel less stressed, so they can feel happier too.” (compound sentence)
“All in all I think friends are important.” (missing comma)
“All in all, I believe that having a true friend makes a big difference.” (linking phrase + stronger vocab)

Stage aim: To show students what substantive editing looks like and preview the five station focus areas.

Time: 4 min. Interaction: T-Ss.

For each pair: read the rough version, ask what is wrong, then click to reveal the edited version. Discuss what changed. After all four pairs, board the five focus areas: compound sentences (pairs 1, 3), linking phrases (pair 4), complete sentences (pair 2), paragraph organisation (briefly), flow (overall). Explain the station rotation.

Let’s Review

Compound Sentences

Two S-V pairs joined by a comma + coordinator

I have a close friend, and she always listens to me.

Time: ~50 s. Interaction: T-Ss.

Ask: “What are the two S-V pairs?” (I have, she listens). “What coordinator joins them?” (and). “Could we use a different coordinator?” (so, but).

Paragraph Structure

Hook — A question or statement to grab attention

Reasons + Examples — One idea per paragraph, with a personal story

Conclusion — Final opinion that broadens to a wider point

Time: ~50 s. Interaction: T-Ss.

Ask: “Point to each part in your own draft. Does your hook grab attention? Do your reasons have examples? Does your conclusion broaden?”

Linking Phrases

Opinion — In my opinion, I believe that, From my point of view

Adding — Also, In addition, Moreover

Contrasting — However, Although, On the other hand

Concluding — All in all, To sum up, In conclusion

Time: ~50 s. Interaction: T-Ss.

Read each category. Ask: “Call out one phrase from each category. Which category does ‘Moreover’ belong to?” (adding).

Complete Sentences

Fragment — Missing S-V pair. Fix: add a subject or verb.

Run-on — Two S-V pairs with no conjunction. Fix: add comma + coordinator.

Comma splice — Two S-V pairs joined only by a comma. Fix: add a coordinator.

Time: ~50 s. Interaction: T-Ss.

Display each error type. Ask: “What’s wrong here? How would you fix it?” Students call out the fix.

Flow

Does your article read smoothly from start to finish? Do the ideas connect in a logical order?

Time: ~50 s. Interaction: T-Ss.

Explain: “Flow is the hardest to define but the easiest to feel. Read your article to yourself and ask: does it make sense from beginning to end? If a sentence feels out of place, move it or remove it.”

Let’s Edit

Station 1 — Sentence Doctor

Focus: Compound sentences

Task: Circle coordinators. Count S-V pairs. Add missing commas before coordinators. Need at least 2 compound sentences.

Partner verify: Check for missed commas and coordinators. Does each compound sentence have two complete S-V pairs?

Part of 24-min station rotation. Interaction: Ss-Ss.

Students self-edit first, then partner verifies. 4 min per station, then rotate clockwise. Station signs at each table have expanded task details. This slide is the pre-rotation explanation.

Station 2 — Paragraph Architect

Focus: Article structure

Task: Draw lines between your paragraphs. Label each section: [HOOK], [REASONS + EXAMPLES], [CONCLUSION]. If any section is missing, note it in the margin.

Partner verify: Can you identify all three parts in your partner’s draft? Is each paragraph about one main idea?

Part of 24-min station rotation. Interaction: Ss-Ss.

Students self-edit first, then partner verifies. 4 min per station, then rotate clockwise.

Station 3 — Link Master

Focus: Transitions and cohesive devices

Task: Underline every linking phrase. Label each one by category: Op (opinion), Add (adding), Cont (contrasting), Concl (concluding). Check reference pronouns (it, this, they) — is it clear what they refer to?

Partner verify: Check your partner’s category labels. Are they correct? Did they miss any reference pronouns that need fixing?

Part of 24-min station rotation. Interaction: Ss-Ss.

Students self-edit first, then partner verifies. 4 min per station, then rotate clockwise.

Station 4 — Fragment Hunter

Focus: Complete sentences

Task: Mark every sentence: ✓ (correct), F (fragment), RO (run-on), CS (comma splice). Fix every error you find. Pay special attention to sentences starting with “Because.”

Partner verify: Check every label. Do you agree? Read the fixed sentences aloud — do they sound complete?

Part of 24-min station rotation. Interaction: Ss-Ss.

Students self-edit first, then partner verifies. 4 min per station, then rotate clockwise.

Station 5 — Sound Check

Focus: Overall flow

Task: Read your entire article aloud to your partner. Read slowly and point to each word. Listen for anything that sounds wrong — missing words, awkward phrasing, unclear ideas.

Partner verify: After they finish, tell them one sentence that worked really well, and one place where the meaning was hard to follow.

Part of 24-min station rotation. Interaction: Ss-Ss.

Students self-edit first (read aloud), then partner gives feedback. 4 min per station, then rotate clockwise.

Final Polish

Final Version — Checklist

□ At least 2 correctly formed compound sentences

□ Three clear paragraphs (hook, reasons, conclusion)

□ At least 2 different linking phrase categories

□ No fragments, run-ons, or comma splices

□ All station corrections transferred to final version

Stage aim: To give learners time to incorporate all corrections and produce a final version.

Time: 14 min. Interaction: S.

Students write their final version. Remind them to check each item on the checklist after writing. Circulate and support — prompt with questions rather than giving answers. Collect both the marked-up first draft (with station edits visible) and the final version for teacher assessment.
