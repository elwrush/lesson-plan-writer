Headway Pre-Intermediate Pronunciation

Lesson: Connected Speech and Linking. CEFR B1-B2. Duration: 46 minutes. The train image shows boxcars linked together — a visual metaphor for how words connect in natural speech.

Today you will learn to...

  • Evaluate speech against the PET pronunciation scale
  • Use linking to connect words smoothly
  • Use linking to sound more natural and fluent
Aim: To orient students. Elicit: "What do you know about linking? Have you heard English speakers who sound connected?" Connect to PET: linking moves students up the scale.

PET Pronunciation Scale

5 Easy to understand. Good rhythm and intonation. Word stress usually correct.
4 Between Band 3 and Band 5.
3 Mostly understandable. Some control of sounds, stress, and intonation.
2 Between Band 1 and Band 3.
1 Mostly understandable, but limited control of pronunciation features.
0 Below Band 1.
Aim: To familiarise learners with the PET pronunciation scale. Timing: 5 min. Display the scale. Explain bands 0-5. Elicit: "What do examiners listen for?" Then click to reveal: Band 3 ("Most of you are here"), Band 5 ("Your target").
