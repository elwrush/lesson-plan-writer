What is a Table Read?

Actors sit around a table, read the script aloud – no costumes, no sets, no cameras.

WATCH THEIR VOICES. How do these actors show emotion and character using ONLY their voice?

Ask: “Has anyone seen a table read before?” Elicit or explain. Set the task: “We’re going to watch the SAME scene twice. First, the table read — voices only. Watch their VOICES. How do they show emotion without body language or camera work?”

The Secret

getting into a role

He’s not being “Aaron Paul” – he’s being Jesse. The character frees him.

“Why does Aaron Paul’s table read work, even without acting? He’s not being ‘Aaron Paul’ — he’s being Jesse. The character frees him from self-consciousness. When you’re a character, you’re not worried about your own mistakes. You’re worried about what the character wants.”

Voice Tips

PACE

Not too fast. Give the words room to land.

VOLUME

Loud doesn’t mean angry. Soft doesn’t mean weak.

PACE — Johnny speaks slower than Dally. VOLUME — Cherry is loud when she’s angry, soft when she’s kind.

More Voice Tips

EMOTION

How does your character FEEL right now? Let that into your voice.

PAUSES

A pause is powerful. It says “this matters.”

EMOTION — how does your character feel right now? PAUSES — let the important words breathe.
