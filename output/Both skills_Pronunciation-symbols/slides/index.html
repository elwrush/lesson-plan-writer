Why do we need this alphabet?

English word: water

Thai pronunciation: "wa-dter"

/ˈwɔːtə(r)/

The phonemic script shows every sound — no guessing!

Write 'water' on the board. Ask Ss how they say it in Thai. Show the IPA: /ˈwɔːtə(r)/. Point out: — stress mark ˈ on first syllable — schwa /ə/ in the second syllable — /r/ at the end (some dialects drop it) Frame: This alphabet removes all the guesswork.

Voiced & Unvoiced Consonants

Put your hand on your throat. /p/ — no vibration. /b/ — vibration.

UnvoicedVoicedPairs

Work through pairs 1-5. For each: demonstrate voiced/unvoiced with throat hand test. Ss repeat chorally and individually. Ask: Which of these exist in Thai? (/p/, /b/, /t/, /d/, /k/, /g/, /f/, /s/ exist; /v/, /z/ don't)

More Pairs

UnvoicedVoicedPairs
/θ//ð/think — this
/ʃ//ʒ/shoe — measure
/tʃ//dʒ/chicken — judge

/θ/, /ð/, /ʃ/, /ʒ/ have no Thai equivalent. /tʃ/ and /dʒ/ do.

θ/ð/ʃ/ʒ don't exist in Thai at all. tʃ (cho-chang) and dʒ (jo-jan) DO exist in Thai. Spend extra time on /θ/ and /ð/ — hardest for Thai learners. Show tongue position: between teeth for θ/ð, behind ridge for ʃ/ʒ.

Vowels & Stress

Short vs long vowels — the length can change the word!

/ɪ/ sit · /iː/ seat  |  /ʊ/ pull · /uː/ pool  |  /e/ bed · /æ/ cat

Schwa /ə/ — the lazy sound

It's in every unstressed syllable. E.g. water → /ˈwɔːtə(r)/

Stress mark ˈ

Tells you which syllable is louder: /ˈwɔːtə(r)/

Move through vowel pairs. Contrast the Thai tone system with English vowel length. Demonstrate schwa: say 'water' naturally — the second vowel is barely there. Show stress: say WATER vs waTER — which sounds right? The mark tells you.

Let's Practise

Model — Sentence 1

Tom likes writing poetry.

/tɒm laɪks 'raɪtɪŋ 'pəʊətri/

Schwa: /pəʊətri/ — two schwas in one word!

Stress: 'raɪtɪŋ — first syllable is louder

Same as Thai: /t/, /ɒ/, /m/, /l/, /k/, /s/

Read the IPA aloud slowly, pointing to each symbol. Tom — t-ɒ-m. Likes — l-aɪ-k-s. Writing — r-aɪ-t-ɪ-ŋ. Poetry — p-əʊ-ə-t-r-i. Ask: Where is the schwa? Where is the stress mark? Which symbols are the same in Thai? Which are different?

Sentence 2

/dʒeɪnz ɡɒt ə 'frendli braʊn pet kæt/

Jane's got a friendly brown pet cat.

Give Ss 30 seconds to decode silently. Elicit the English sentence from a student. Focus: /dʒ/ and /kæt/ — 'cat' with /æ/, not Thai แคท.

Sentence 3

/suː wɒz fɑːst əˈsliːp wen ðə ˈbɜːɡləz keɪm/

Sue was fast asleep when the burglars came.

Give Ss 30 seconds to decode silently. Elicit from a student. Highlight: schwa in 'asleep' /əˈsliːp/ and /ð/ in 'the' /ðə/.

Sentence 4

/dʒəʊ bɔːt ə ˈbɒtəl əv hʌŋˈɡeəriən waɪn lɑːst naɪt/

Joe bought a bottle of Hungarian wine last night.

Give Ss 30 seconds to decode silently. Elicit from a student. Focus: /hʌŋˈɡeəriən/ — three syllables, stress on /ɡeə/.

Sentence 5

/lɪz wɒnts tə biː rɪtʃ ənd ˈfeɪməs wʌn deɪ/

Liz wants to be rich and famous one day.

Give Ss 30 seconds to decode silently. Elicit from a student. Note: schwa in 'famous' /ˈfeɪməs/ and /wʌn/ for 'one'.
