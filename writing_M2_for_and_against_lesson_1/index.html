How can I express what I think about an issue?
Let's learn how to write a for/against essay.
Ask: What do you think this lesson is about? Point at the angel and devil on the splash.

Why is this lesson important?

  • Every day, people argue for and against things.
  • A clear opinion makes your writing powerful.
  • Essays and exams ask for your opinion.
Frame: phones, apps, and school rules all have two sides. Both sides matter.
