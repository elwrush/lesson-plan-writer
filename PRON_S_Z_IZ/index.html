Can you hear the -s?
Let's train your ears and your mouth.

Why is this lesson important?

  • If you drop the -s, your listener can't tell one cat from many cats.
  • These three little sounds make your English clear.

What things do you have?

Tell your partner two things you own.

The plural rule

After a voiceless sound: /p/, /t/, /k/, /f/-s says 
After a voiced sound: /b/, /d/, /g/, /v/, /l/, /m/, /n/-s says 
After a hiss: /s/, /z/, /ʃ/, /tʃ/-s says 
 

The plural rule

After a voiceless sound: /p/, /t/, /k/, /f/-s says/s/ — books
After a voiced sound: /b/, /d/, /g/, /v/, /l/, /m/, /n/-s says/z/ — dogs
After a hiss: /s/, /z/, /ʃ/, /tʃ/-s says/ɪz/ — glasses
/ɪz/ adds a syllable. Glass = 1. Glasses = 2.
