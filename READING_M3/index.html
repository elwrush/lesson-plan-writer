How one round works

1. Predict — only the Predictor speaks.

2. Read the chunk silently.

3. Clarify, Question, Summarise — in turn.

4. Rotate roles. Next chunk.

Walk the round: Predict before reading, everyone reads silently, then the three after-roles speak in turn, then rotate.

Your four roles

Predict before you read. The others talk after you read.

PredictorLook at the heading. Guess the topic.“The heading says… so I think…”
ClarifierSay what confused you. Fix it together.“I didn’t understand when it said…”
QuestionerAsk a real “I wonder…” question.“I wonder… why…”
SummariserSay the main idea in one sentence.“The main point is…”
Recap the four roles with the exact definitions and sentence starters. The Predictor works before reading; the other three build on each other after reading.
