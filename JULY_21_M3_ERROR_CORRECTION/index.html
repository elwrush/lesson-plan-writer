How can I make my writing less messy?
Let's find out.

Ask: What makes writing look messy? Why do small mistakes matter?

Why is this lesson important?

  • Most of your writing errors come from punctuation and capitalisation.
  • Fixing these will make your writing clearer and more professional.

Task 1 — Recognise

Instructions

Each sentence has errors in punctuation or capitalisation.

Circle each error.

Write the correction above it.

Demo on the board: Show how to circle 'i' and write 'I' above it. Show worksheet task 1.

Example

i went to the beach yesterday.

CCQ: Why is 'i' wrong? What should it be? (Answer: 'I' is always capitalised.)

Example

I went to the beach yesterday.

The pronoun I is always capitalised.

Task 2 — Recall

Each box shows an error from Task 1.

Explain the rule that was broken.

Write one complete sentence.

Demo: 'i' → 'The pronoun I must always be capitalised.' Give 5 min for all four rules.

Rule — The pronoun I

The pronoun I must always be capitalised.

Never write i.

CCQ: What about 'my', 'me', 'mine'? (Those are not capitalised. Only 'I'.)

Rule — Common nouns

Don't capitalise common nouns.

Only proper nouns (names, places) need capitals.

teacher, manager, students → lowercase.

CCQ: Is 'English' a common or proper noun? (Proper - nationality/language.) CCQ: Is 'manager' proper? (No - it's a job title.)

Rule — Comma spacing

Never put a space before a comma.

Always put a space after a comma.

Chiang Mai, Thailand ✓

CCQ: Which is correct: 'word , word' or 'word, word'? (Second one.)

Rule — After an introduction

After an introductory phrase, use a comma.

Then use a lowercase letter.

In my opinion, the city council ✓

CCQ: What comes after the comma? (A lowercase letter, not uppercase.)

Rule — Extra information

Use commas around extra information about a person.

The famous author, Stephen King, has written...

CCQ: If we remove 'Stephen King', is the sentence still complete? (Yes.) That's how you know it needs commas.

Rule — Connecting with However

Use a semicolon before however.

Use a comma after it.

...the conference ; however, she couldn't...

CCQ: What punctuation goes BEFORE 'however'? (Semicolon.) What goes AFTER? (Comma.)

Task 3 — Use

Write a paragraph about a difficult decision you had to make.

Focus on correct punctuation and capitalisation.

~70 words.

Give 8 min. Circulate and help. Remind students to check their work before handing in.

Checklist

  • A sentence starting with although, because, or while
  • A sentence with extra information in commas
  • Correct comma before and or but
  • The word however used correctly

Before they start, review each item. Ask for an example sentence for each.
