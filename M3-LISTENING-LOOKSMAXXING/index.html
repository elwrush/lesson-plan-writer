mainstream

Fifteen years ago, almost nobody ate vegan food. Now every supermarket sells it — it has gone mainstream.

“When something is mainstream, is it popular or unpopular? (Popular — most people know about it)”

/ˈsuːdəʊˌsaɪəns/

pseudoscience

Some websites say drinking lemon water melts 10 kilos in a week. It sounds scientific but it is not — it is pseudoscience.

“Does pseudoscience use real evidence or fake-sounding claims? (Fake-sounding claims)”

/səbˈdʒektɪv/

subjective

My favourite song is the best ever — but my best friend hates it. What makes music good is subjective.

“If something is subjective, does everyone agree on it? (No — it depends on the person)”
