#  {#slide-vocab-mainstream}

/ˈmeɪnstriːm/

[**mainstream**]{.fragment .answer-reveal}

[Fifteen years ago, almost nobody ate vegan food. Now every supermarket sells it — it has gone [**mainstream**]{.box}.]{.fragment .white-reveal}

::: notes
"When something is mainstream, is it popular or unpopular? (Popular — most people know about it)"
:::

#  {#slide-vocab-pseudoscience}

/ˈsuːdəʊˌsaɪəns/

[**pseudoscience**]{.fragment .answer-reveal}

[Some websites say drinking lemon water melts 10 kilos in a week. It sounds scientific but it is not — it is [**pseudoscience**]{.box}.]{.fragment .white-reveal}

::: notes
"Does pseudoscience use real evidence or fake-sounding claims? (Fake-sounding claims)"
:::

#  {#slide-vocab-subjective}

/səbˈdʒektɪv/

[**subjective**]{.fragment .answer-reveal}

[My favourite song is the best ever — but my best friend hates it. What makes music good is [**subjective**]{.box}.]{.fragment .white-reveal}

::: notes
"If something is subjective, does everyone agree on it? (No — it depends on the person)"
:::
