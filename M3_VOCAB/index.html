What skills do you need for work?
Let's find out.
Greet students. Ask: What skills do you need for a good job? What do you see in the picture?

Why is this lesson important?

  • These words help you talk about jobs and school.
  • You will need them at interviews.
  • They help you understand how people make a good impression.

QuestionWhy is Lily at International School Bangkok?
AnswerShe is having an interview to join the school.

Transcript“I really hope I get a place at your school.”

Listen again

Answer the questions.

1. What does the director say about first impressions?

2. What is Lily's best skill?

3. What happened with the school play?

4. What does Lily need to work on?

Answers (1/4)

Question1. What does the director say about first impressions?
AnswerThey start with a handshake. A good first impression matters.
ExplanationLily agrees and says she makes eye contact and smiles.
Transcript“We like to start with a handshake. A good first impression matters.”
Check the answer. Point out handshake, make eye contact from the unit.

Answers (2/4)

Question2. What is Lily's best skill?
AnswerCommunication. She listens well and enjoys talking to people.
ExplanationShe also says she is a team player who stays calm under pressure.
Transcript“My best skill is communication. I listen well.”
Check the answer. Point out communication and team player from the vocabulary.

Answers (3/4)

Question3. What happened with the school play?
AnswerIt lost its director. Lily came up with a new plan, and they acted out all the scenes in one week.
ExplanationSome friends dropped out, but she faced up to the challenge. Hard work pays off.
Transcript“I came up with a new plan, and we acted out all the scenes in one week.”
Check the answer. Point out the multi-word verbs: came up with, acted out, dropped out, faced up to, pays off.

Answers (4/4)

Question4. What does Lily need to work on?
AnswerHer time management. She leaves big tasks to the last minute.
ExplanationThe director says patience helps and time management is a skill you can learn.
Transcript“I sometimes leave big tasks to the last minute.”
Check the answer. Point out time management and patience from the vocabulary.

Exercise 5 — Answers

If you spend time in Bangkok, you should know about how people interact. Friends usually shake hands or kiss each other on the cheek when they meet.

When people make small talk, they usually chat about the weather or sports like basketball or soccer.

If you want to make a good impression, wearing smart clothes will impress people. But it's also important to smile and make eye contact.

If you don't look at people directly, they won't trust you.
