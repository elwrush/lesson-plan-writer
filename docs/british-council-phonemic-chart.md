## Key Rules for Transcription

1. **British English is non-rhotic**: No /r/ at the end of a syllable.
   - "teacher" = /ˈtiːtʃə/  NOT /ˈtitʃər/
   - "car" = /kɑː/  NOT /kɑr/
   - "here" = /hɪə/  NOT /hɪr/

2. **/e/ NOT /ɛ/**: The DRESS vowel is always /e/.
   - "echo" = /ˈekəʊ/  NOT /ˈɛkoʊ/
   - "bread" = /bred/  NOT /brɛd/

3. **/əʊ/ NOT /oʊ/**: The GOAT diphthong is /əʊ/.
   - "echo" = /ˈekəʊ/  NOT /ˈɛkoʊ/
   - "go" = /ɡəʊ/  NOT /ɡoʊ/

4. **/ɒ/ NOT /ɑ/**: The LOT vowel is /ɒ/.
   - "hot" = /hɒt/  NOT /hɑt/
   - "misogynist" = /mɪˈsɒdʒɪnɪst/  NOT /mɪˈsɑdʒɪnɪst/

5. **/ɪə/ NOT /ɪr/**: The NEAR diphthong is /ɪə/.
   - "here" = /hɪə/  NOT /hɪr/
   - "manosphere" = /ˈmænəsfɪə/  NOT /ˈmænəˌsfɪr/

6. **/eə/ NOT /ɛr/**: The SQUARE diphthong is /eə/.
   - "hair" = /heə/  NOT /hɛr/
   - "there" = /ðeə/  NOT /ðɛr/

7. **Stress marks**: Primary stress before the syllable: /ˈ/.
   - Secondary stress (before a syllable below word-level): /ˌ/.
   - "manosphere" = /ˈmænəsfɪə/ — no secondary stress needed
