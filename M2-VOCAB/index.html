Listen again

Answer the questions.

1. How does the school teach students?

2. How does a student get a grade?

3. When can students use phones?

4. What happens in the final year?

Answers (1/4)

Question1. How does the school teach students?
AnswerWith small classes and plenty of discussion.
ExplanationMs. Clark describes the teaching method first.
Transcript“We use small classes and plenty of discussion.”
Check the answer. Elicit: small classes and discussion.

Answers (2/4)

Question2. How does a student get a grade?
AnswerThrough continuous assessment, from work all year.
ExplanationGrades come from assignments written every month, not one exam.
Transcript“We use continuous assessment. A student's grade comes from their work all year.”
Check the answer. Point out the word assessment from the vocabulary.

Answers (3/4)

Question3. When can students use phones?
AnswerAt break time. They are banned during lessons.
ExplanationPhones are allowed only between classes.
Transcript“We allow phones at break time. We ban them during lessons.”
Check the answer. Point out allow and ban from the vocabulary.

Answers (4/4)

Question4. What happens in the final year?
AnswerStudents do work experience, three weeks in a real workplace.
ExplanationThe school sends students to a real job before they finish.
Transcript“In the final year. They spend three weeks in a real workplace.”
Check the answer. Point out work experience from the vocabulary.
