Page 132, Exercise 1

QuestionWhat is the function of each paragraph?
AnswerThe introduction asks the question. Paragraph 2 argues for individual sports. Paragraph 3 argues for team sports. The conclusion gives the writer's opinion.
ExplanationEach paragraph has one job. The essay argues both sides before it chooses.

Page 132, Exercise 1

QuestionWhat is the writer's overall opinion?
AnswerTeam sports are better for young people.
ExplanationThe conclusion says young people learn more from playing on a team.

Page 132, Exercise 1

QuestionWhat are the two meanings of "there is no 'I' in team"?
AnswerLiteral: the word team has no letter i. Real meaning: the team matters more than any one player.
ExplanationThe writer uses it to say teamwork is more valuable than individual success.

Strategy: Compare and contrast

DoPresent two ideas. Argue each in turn.
WhyThe reader sees both sides fairly.
HowEnd with your preference and why.
