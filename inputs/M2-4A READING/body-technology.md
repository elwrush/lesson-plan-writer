# Body Technology

Body technology is technology that helps the human body work better. It helps people do more.

**Powered exoskeletons**

Powered exoskeletons help people lift heavy things. They can help people lift very heavy things — up to 20 times their own weight. They also help people with injuries. People who cannot walk can stand up and walk again.

Exoskeletons are already helping people. In some factories, workers wear them. They help the workers lift things more easily and stay safe.

**Mechanoprint**

Scientists create a second skin called "mechanoprint". It works like a fingerprint. It is an electronic skin that can tell who you are. It can also tell if you are healthy or sick.

The scientists who created mechanoprint say it can give people a more secure way to use their phones and computers. It is also very thin and comfortable to wear.

**Bionic contact lenses**

Bionic contact lenses help people with eye injuries. They can zoom in and out. People who have trouble seeing can use them to see better. The lenses are very small and fit inside the eye.

Some scientists are also working on bionic contact lenses that can take photos and record videos. The person wearing them can control the lenses with their eyes.

**Running blades**

Some prosthetic legs help people run faster than ever. They can bend like a real leg. This means people can do sports like running with no problems.

Oscar Pistorius was the first person to run in the Olympics with prosthetic legs. He showed that bionic legs can be as good as real legs. Since then, more and more people have started to use them.

**Bionic eye**

This kind of bionic eye helps people with eye injuries. People who cannot see well might be able to read again. The bionic eye sends signals to the brain. These signals help the brain understand what the eyes are seeing.

More than 200 people already have a bionic eye. Scientists are working to make them better and more affordable for everyone.
