\### 7. Study the speaking strategy, then practice giving your talk.

Speaking strategy 



Emphasizing important points 



\* 

\*\*Speed\*\* — Speak slowly and clearly to add emphasis. 





\* 

\*\*Volume/stress\*\* — Say the important words more loudly. 





\* 

\*\*Repetition\*\* — If something is important, say it several times in different ways.

Audio Script (1.01) 



\*\*Dad:\*\* How was your day? 



\*\*Mariam:\*\* Good thanks. We didn't have our usual classes because it was International Safer Internet Day. 



\*\*Dad:\*\* Ooh!... I could learn from that. What did you do? 



\*\*Mariam:\*\* We started with a quiz about social media privacy. I got most of the answers right. I manage my privacy settings carefully so only my friends can see things. 



\*\*Dad:\*\* That's good... maybe I need to look at my settings. 



\*\*Mariam:\*\* But I learned something from the quiz. It's important that my friends have strong privacy settings, too - if not, other people can see photos or posts that I'm in. So I need to talk to my friends about this! 



\*\*Dad:\*\* Oh I didn't know that. Yes, that is important. 



\*\*Mariam:\*\* In the next lesson, we had a talk and discussion about online safety when we're away from home. 



\*\*Dad:\*\* What do you mean? 



\*\*Mariam:\*\* Using free hotspots in public places like cafes. You need to be sure that they're safe. Hackers can set up a fake hotspot with a similar name to the one in the cafe and then take your personal information. 



\*\*Dad:\*\* Oh no, I use them all the time! How can I check? 



\*\*Mariam:\*\* Our teacher always asks the people who run the cafe for the real connection name and the IP address. 



\*\*Dad:\*\* Really?... That's a good idea. 



\*\*Mariam:\*\* Then, after lunch we looked at password safety. All my passwords are strong because they're long and have a combination of letters, symbols and numbers. But I've never updated those passwords... or PINs. 



\*\*Dad:\*\* I sometimes update passwords, but I should update my PINs immediately. I've had them for years. 



\*\*Mariam:\*\* It's a bit boring but we all need to do it! The last thing that we did was different. We started a project called the online cyber security challenge. 



\*\*Dad:\*\* What's that? 



\*\*Mariam:\*\* Well, cyber security is about keeping the systems in big organizations safe from hackers. We started by doing code breaking games today. 



\*\*Dad:\*\* Sounds great. 



\*\*Mariam:\*\* I'm really into it, and I'm pretty good at it already! I need to keep practicing if I want to be an expert in cyber security one day. 



\*\*Dad:\*\* Well that's your future job decided!
