# Unit 1

* When we speak quickly, a word that begins with a vowel sound is linked to the consonant sound at the end of the word before it:
  * Greek\_alphabet
  * modern\_email
* Linking also happens if a consonant is usually silent at the end of a word:
  * **/w/** new\_idea
  * **/r/** computer\_information
* The letter *y* links to the following vowel sound as a **/j/**:
  * **/j/** 14th-century\_English
