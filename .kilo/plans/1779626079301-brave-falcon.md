## Critical Design Rules (from skill — not legacy slideshows)

1. **Title slide**: `#1a1a2e` solid bg, NO logo, NO `data-background-image`, topic + CEFR badge + strap subheader. No date/teacher/materials.
2. **Objective**: 3 "I can..." statements, motivational footer about PET reading test.
3. **Lead-in**: `#1a1a2e` solid bg, one open question. No image.
4. **Transitions**: `#c0392b` red bg, heading ONLY. No subheader text paragraphs. No icons.
5. **Task slides**: dark bg + `data-timer`, brief student-facing instructions, `<aside class="notes">` with full procedure.
6. **Answer slides**: `#1e7e34` green bg, `<table class="answer-table">`, fragments on answer cells (NOT highlight-green/red). Use `<i class="fa-solid fa-check">` / `<i class="fa-solid fa-times">` for check/cross — NEVER Unicode ✓✗.
7. **Summary**: "What you can do now" + "I can..." statements with fa-check.
8. **End**: `#2c3e50`, topic + CEFR.
9. **No icons** on any slide (removed per AGENTS.md).
10. **No Pixabay auto-downloads** — solid colors only.
11. **B1 authorial voice**: direct "you" imperatives, ≤15 words/sentence, no "identify"/"distinguish"/"infer".
12. **Speaker notes**: ALL non-transition, non-end slides get `<aside class="notes">` with stage aim, time, interaction, full procedure.
