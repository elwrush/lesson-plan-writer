## ERRANT Analysis (from `C:\PROJECTS\ERRANT-ANALYSIS\PDF\M3-3A-assignment-2\31-05-26-M3-3A-combined.pdf`)

14 students, dominant error categories:
1. **Comma splices** (most common) — e.g., "My mom was okay with it too so, I can play piano"
2. **Run-on sentences** — e.g., "When I wear it I feel more confident it does give me more security"
3. **Sentence fragments** — e.g., "My most prized posession. Is my watches."
4. Subject-verb agreement — e.g., "My soccer gloves is very important to me"
5. Verb tense — past/present mixing
6. Spelling — "posession" → "possession"

## Lesson 1: Compound Sentences with and, but, or, so (pp 30-32)

**Shape:** C (Test-Teach-Test)
**Duration:** 46 minutes
**CEFR:** B1
**Topic:** "Compound Sentences"

### Stage Architecture

| # | Stage | Aim | Procedure | Time | Int |
|---|-------|-----|-----------|------|-----|
| 1 | Lead-in — Grammar Feedback Review | To activate awareness of writing errors and build motivation for the grammar focus | Distribute individual ERRANT feedback. Ss review their errors silently (3 min). Teacher highlights class patterns: comma splices and run-ons are the big ones. "Your next CA is a narrative paragraph. But first, we need to learn how to join sentences correctly." Write goal on board. | 5 | T-Ss |
| 2 | Test 1 — Diagnostic (Compound Sentences) | To diagnose baseline ability to combine simple sentences into compounds | Display 6 pairs of simple sentences. Ss join each pair using and/but/or/so. Bespoke — NOT from textbook. Individual (4 min). Pair check (2 min). Teacher notes: correct coordinator choice? comma placement? | 6 | S, Ss-Ss |
| 3 | Teach — Coordinators: and, but, or, so | To clarify the meaning and function of coordinating conjunctions in compound sentences | Display compound sentence formula: SV, and SV. Walk through each coordinator using model sentences from the textbook: and (similar), but (contrast), so (result), or (choice). Use auto-animate for formula reveal (SV V → SV, and SV). Comma rule: comma BEFORE coordinator, never after. | 10 | T-Ss |
| 4 | Test 2 — Controlled Practice: Practice 2A + 2B | To give structured practice distinguishing simple from compound sentences | Ss complete Practice 2A (circle coordinators in the folktale) + 2B (label CS/SS, add commas). Individual (8 min). Pair check (3 min). Display answers with fragment reveals. | 11 | S, Ss-Ss, T-Ss |
| 5 | Test 3 — Freer Practice: Practice 2C | To give extended practice choosing coordinators and writing compound sentences | Practice 2C: 8 sentence pairs to combine. Individual writing (6 min). Pair compare + display model answers (4 min). Address multiple acceptable answers. | 10 | S, Ss-Ss, T-Ss |
| 6 | Wrap-up | To consolidate key learning and preview the CA writing task | Quick review: "What makes a compound sentence?" (SV, coordinator SV). "Where does the comma go?" (before coordinator). "What does so signal?" (result). Preview: "Next lesson — comma rules and freewriting to plan your CA paragraph." | 4 | T-Ss |

**Total:** 46 minutes ✓

## Lesson 2: Comma Rules and Freewriting (pp 33-35)

**Shape:** C (Test-Teach-Test)
**Duration:** 46 minutes
**CEFR:** B1
**Topic:** "Comma Rules and Freewriting"

### Stage Architecture

| # | Stage | Aim | Procedure | Time | Int |
|---|-------|-----|-----------|------|-----|
| 1 | Lead-in — Review + Practice 2D | To retrieve compound sentence knowledge from L1 and bridge into comma rules | Quick review: elicit compound sentence formula and 4 coordinators (2 min). Ss complete Practice 2D (write 5 original compound sentences following directions) as warm-up (5 min). Pair share — partner checks for comma+coordinator (2 min). | 9 | T-Ss, S, Ss-Ss |
| 2 | Test 1 — Diagnostic (Comma Rules) | To diagnose baseline knowledge of comma placement rules | Display 6 sentences with missing/misplaced commas. Ss add commas where needed. Covers all 3 rules (time order signals, compound sentences, series). Bespoke — NOT from textbook. Individual (3 min). Pair check (2 min). Teacher notes error patterns. | 5 | S, Ss-Ss |
| 3 | Teach — Three Comma Rules | To explicitly teach the comma rules for time order signals, compound sentences, and series | Walk through each rule with textbook examples. Rule 1: comma after time order signal (except then/soon/now). Rule 2: comma before coordinator in compound sentences. Rule 3: comma between items in a series of 3+. Use auto-animate for rule reveal. | 8 | T-Ss |
| 4 | Test 2 — Controlled Practice: Practice 3A | To give structured practice applying comma rules | Ss complete Practice 3A (items 1-12): add commas. Individual (6 min). Pair check (3 min). Whole-class feedback on screen with fragment reveals. Highlight tricky items: series of 3+, dates, compound sentences with compound subjects. | 10 | S, Ss-Ss, T-Ss |
| 5 | Teach/Test 3 — Freewriting Try It Out! | To introduce freewriting as a prewriting technique and practice it in the CA context | Display freewriting model from textbook (p.34). Explain: "Write without stopping, don't worry about errors." Ss freewrite for 10 min on a memorable event (their CA topic). "Use compound sentences with correct commas." Timer on screen. Optional: pair share a sentence you're proud of. | 11 | T-Ss, S |
| 6 | Wrap-up | To consolidate comma rules and connect freewriting to the CA paragraph | "Name the three comma rules." (time signals, compound, series). "What's freewriting?" (writing without stopping). "Your freewrite is step 1 — next you'll organize and write your first draft." | 3 | T-Ss |

**Total:** 46 minutes ✓

## Key Design Decisions (from AGENTS.md and M2 precedent)

1. **Bespoke diagnostic tests** — Shape C requires teacher-written diagnostics, NOT textbook exercises. L1 diagnostic: 6 sentence pairs to join with and/but/or/so. L2 diagnostic: 6 sentences with missing commas covering all 3 rules.
2. **One step per slide** — pedagogical strategy slides follow one-step-per-slide pattern
3. **Auto-animate for transformation reveals** — SV V → SV, and SV morph (L1), comma rule reveals (L2)
4. **Fragment fade-up for answers** — teacher controls pacing, one row per click
5. **Green answer slides** — all text white/yellow only, no gray/muted colors
6. **Max 3 items per answer slide** — split exercises with >5 items across two slides
7. **Pedagogical slides top-aligned** — `align-self: flex-start; padding-top: 30px`
8. **No icons** — Font Awesome CDN loaded but no `<i>` elements on slides
9. **Stable slide IDs** — kebab-case prefixes matching lesson stage
10. **ERRANT errors as teaching examples** — real student errors from M3-3A feedback woven into lead-in and teach slides (anonymized)
