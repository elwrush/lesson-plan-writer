**Why auto-animate here:** The user said "use auto-animate when students need to see how language is put together." The PET scale auto-animate shows how Organization and Language relate to the overall score. The sentence definition auto-animate shows how the three criteria (S+V+complete thought) assemble into a complete definition.

**Why NOT auto-animate elsewhere:**
- Diagnostic slides (5–6): Students work on content — no animation needed
- Capitalization rules (12–13): Expository content — teacher reads, students reference
- Task instructions (14, 17): Static instructions — students refer back during work
- Answer slides (15–16, 18): Fragment reveals, NOT auto-animate
- Command sentences (11): Standalone concept — one slide is sufficient
