## Key Design Decisions

1. **Splash slide before title**: Full-bleed image of "Bye Bye Binary" placard with no content — primes topic visually before any information appears.

2. **Vocabulary grouped 2-per-slide**: 10 words in 5 slides (instead of 10 individual slides) to fit within the 7-minute pre-listening stage. Each word gets phonemic → word → context.

3. **YouTube embedded with start/end times**: Each Part's task slide has an embedded YouTube iframe cued to the appropriate segment. Answers include "Replay segment" notes so the teacher can scroll back.

4. **One answer per comprehension Q**: Each question gets its own slide with the question visible on entry and the answer revealing via fragment. WHY includes a direct transcript quote.

5. **Scaffolded vs. free discussion**: Q1 on Think-Pair-Share has the 4-step structure displayed. Q2 has no structure — just the question.

**Pedagogical Justification**: This is a protracted B2 listening task broken into three manageable segments (Segmenting principle). The two-tier approach respects Vygotsky's ZPD — emerging learners get outline scaffolding, established learners work independently. Comprehension at each pause (chokepoints) with answer reveal + transcript quote reinforces Temporal Contiguity (text + audio evidence simultaneously). Discussion techniques are front-loaded before the Think-Pair-Share so students have functional language to use during pair work — this is a form of task-readiness scaffolding that lowers affective filter for Year 9 students dealing with sensitive content.
