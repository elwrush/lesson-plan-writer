# Three Problems {#pron-issues}

[**Flat voice** — no rise or fall in pitch. Everything sounds the same.]{.fragment .white-reveal}

[**Overstressing final syllables** — every word ends with the same force.]{.fragment .white-reveal}

[**Dropping consonants** — /t/, /d/, /z/, /k/ don’t get heard.]{.fragment .white-reveal}

::: notes
