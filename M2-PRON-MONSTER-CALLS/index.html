Your voice is your instrument.
Let’s tune it.

Three Problems

Flat voice – no rise or fall in pitch. Everything sounds the same.

Overstressing final syllables – every word ends with the same force.

Dropping consonants – /t/, /d/, /z/, /k/ don’t get heard.

Click through each problem. Elicit examples from students. Ask: “Which one is YOUR biggest problem?”

Exercise A

Pronounce Every Sound

Transition: “Now we’re going to fix these problems. Open your worksheets.”

Skill: Pronouncing every consonant

You have 5 sentences on your worksheet.

Each boxed letter is a sound you MUST pronounce.

Read each sentence aloud.

Don’t drop any /t/, /d/, /z/, or /k/.

Listening

A Monster Calls – Chapters 4 & 5

“Now listen to the audiobook. Read along in your worksheet. Pay attention to how the narrator pronounces every word.”

Read along in your worksheet.

How does the narrator pronounce their words?

Does he drop any sounds?

Exercise B

Dialog – The Monster and Conor

“Now let’s read the dialog. Half the class is the Monster, half is Conor. Swap after the first read.”

Read the dialog in pairs.

Pay special attention to the pronunciation markers.

You are not you.

You are the Monster.

You are Conor.

That’s your voice. Make it heard.
