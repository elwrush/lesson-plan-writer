Opinion — Reason — Example

Strategy: The PREP Formula

P — PositionSay your opinion first. In my opinion…
R — ReasonGive one reason. One reason is…
E — ExampleAdd an example. For example…
P — PositionRepeat your opinion. That’s why…

No 'because' in the first sentence.

Your worksheet shows the same formula.

Try it: A weak answer

Should school start later in the morning?
 Yes, I think that's good. I'm tired in the morning. It would help me.

Try it: The PREP answer

Should school start later in the morning?
 In my opinion, school should start later. One reason is that students need more sleep. For example, teenagers need about nine hours of sleep. That's why a later start would help us learn.
