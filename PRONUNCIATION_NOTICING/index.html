Why is this lesson important?

Your listener can't understand words if you drop the /t/, /d/ and /Id/ endings.

The sounds at the end of words.

Answers: Part E

QuestionWhich word is the past tense?
Answerwanted and played. And night ends with /t/.
ExplanationDropped -ed hides the past. Want is present. Wanted is past.
