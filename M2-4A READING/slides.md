#  {#slide-auto-1 data-autoslide="2500" data-transition="slide-in slide-out"}

**Body Technology**

Technology that helps the human body work better and do more.

#  {#slide-auto-2 data-autoslide="2500" data-transition="slide-in slide-out"}

**Exoskeletons** help people lift heavy things — up to 20 times their own weight.

#  {#slide-auto-3 data-autoslide="2500" data-transition="slide-in slide-out"}

**Mechanoprint** is a second skin that works like a fingerprint and can tell who you are.

#  {#slide-auto-4 data-autoslide="2500" data-transition="slide-in slide-out"}

**Bionic contact lenses** help people with eye injuries and can zoom in and out.

#  {#slide-auto-5 data-autoslide="2500" data-transition="slide-in slide-out"}

**Prosthetic legs** bend like a real leg so people can run and do sports.

#  {#slide-auto-6 data-autoslide="2500" data-transition="slide-in slide-out"}

**Bionic eyes** send signals to the brain so people who cannot see might read again.
