Ideas to improve

Reading — Read one short article a day. Summarise it in 2 sentences.

Listening — Watch short videos with English subtitles. Pause and repeat.

Writing — Write 3 sentences every day. Use new vocabulary.

Vocabulary — Use a vocab notebook. Review 5 words each day.

Vocabulary, Grammar & Spelling

Strong vocab, grammar, and spelling help you in ALL skills.

Reading — You understand more words = you read faster.

Writing — Correct spelling and grammar = clearer ideas.

Listening — You recognise words faster = you follow better.

Your finals will test all of this at the end of the year.

Emphasise: these are foundational skills that support everything else.
